About Us..

ABES Engineering College, located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, is a private institution affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. Established in 2000, it has gained recognition for its academic excellence and has been granted autonomous status in 2025. The college offers a diverse range of programs, including B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, and MBA, with a focus on emerging technologies like AI and ML. With a campus spanning 15.77 acres, it features over 50 IoT-enabled laboratories and dedicated residential hostels. ABES is committed to nurturing well-rounded individuals through a vibrant campus life and has a strong placement record, with the highest salary package reaching INR 59.91 LPA in 2025.
